Information

Karate as it was meant to be taught.

EmptyHands is a Traditional KaraTe School. Our practice is based on the classical Shotokan System with some influences from Goju Ryu, Shiatsu (acupressure) & Internal Martial Arts (Tai Chi Chuan).

Our Dojo was founded in 2006 on the ideals of Gichin Funakoshi Hanshi (founder of modern-day Karate). The format of our syllabus has been inspired by the work of the late Hirokazu Kanazawa Sōke with some adaptations.

Our teaching philosophy is person-centred and based upon the fundamentals of KaraTe-Dô: Kihon (basics), Kata (form), Bunkai (application) and Kumite (sparring). We also place great emphasis on the values and beliefs of KaraTe through the study of the Dojo Kun.

We run weekly classes every Thursday from 7pm to 9pm, and advanced classes for our more developed students at our other Dojo in Epping (invite only). We publish our calendar at the end of each year so that every student has ample opportunity to plan their year ahead.

The fundamentals

How every class is built

Each lesson moves through the four fundamentals of Karate-Dō, closing with the Dojo Kun.

基本

Kihon

Basics

Stances, blocks, strikes and kicks — the alphabet of Karate.

型

Kata

Form

Shotokan kata from Heian Shodan upwards, as set out in our syllabus.

分解

Bunkai

Application

Practical application of kata, including self-defence scenarios.

組手

Kumite

Sparring

From pre-arranged gohon kumite to controlled free sparring.
