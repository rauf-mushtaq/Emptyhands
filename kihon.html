基本
Kihon · Basics

The foundation of every technique

Kihon means “basics” or “foundation”. Every class at EmptyHands works through kihon, with techniques practised in lines, moving forward and back, so that each movement becomes precise, relaxed and powerful.

Good kihon is what makes kata and kumite work. The strength of a punch or block comes from the stance beneath it, so students return to the basics at every grade, from white belt to Sensei.

Kihon in the EmptyHands syllabus

  • Oi-zuki and Sanbon-zukiLunge punch and triple lunge punch
  • Gyaku-zuki and Kizami-zukiReverse punch and jab
  • Tate teisho-uchi and Haito-uchiPalm-heel strike and ridge-hand strike
  • Age-uke, Mae-geri, Gyaku-zukiRising block, front snap kick, reverse punch
  • Ude-uke, Juji-uke, Empi-uchi, Uraken-uchiInside block, cross block, elbow strike in horse-riding stance, backfist
  • Uchi-uke, Kizami-zuki, Gyaku-zukiOutside block, jab to the face, reverse punch
  • Gedan-barai and Gyaku-zukiLower block and reverse punch
  • Shuto-uke, Mae-geri, NukiteKnife-hand block in back stance, front kick, spear-hand strike
  • Kake-uke, Haiwan-uke, Morote-ukeHook block, forearm block, augmented forearm block
  • Mae-geri, Keage-geri, Ren-mae-geriFront snap kicks and triple front kicks
  • Mawashi-geri and Yoko kekomi-geriRoundhouse kick and side thrust kick
  • Mikazuki-geri and Ushiro-geriCrescent kick and back kick

Advanced students perform kihon moving forward, back and in four directions. For black belt, every kick is performed without putting the foot down.
