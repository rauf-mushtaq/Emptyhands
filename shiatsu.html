Shiatsu

Finger pressure — acupressure — grounded in Traditional Oriental Medicine, without needles or herbs.

指圧
Information

Healing through touch.

Shiatsu is a powerful Japanese healing therapy. Shiatsu literally means finger pressure, or acupressure.

It is based on the principles and practices of Traditional Oriental Medicine but without the needles or herbs. We specialise in mental wellbeing, self-care and rehabilitation, and run dedicated workshops and one-to-one sessions.

The EmptyHands clinic was founded by Azeem Mushtaq (Sensei) in 2003 after graduating from the British School of Shiatsu-Dô, and is registered under the Shiatsu Society. It provides advice, support and rehabilitation for sports injuries, back problems and trauma. Azeem’s development was supported by the late Rex Lassalle, Shiatsu Master.
