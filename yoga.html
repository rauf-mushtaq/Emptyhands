Yoga

Information

Breath, posture and stillness.

EmptyHands Yoga is principally based on Himalayan Hatha Yoga, developed by Yogi Ashokananda.

We also incorporate teachings from other modalities such as Karate, Tai Chi and Shiatsu to form a practice that helps alleviate everyday stresses. Classes are led by Irm Mushtaq Sensei, founder and teacher of EmptyHands Yoga.

Our drop-in classes run every Thursday from 8pm to 9pm. We also provide dedicated sessions upon request — feel free to contact us for further information or booking.
