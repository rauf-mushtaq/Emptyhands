Our story

Founded by Azeem Mushtaq (Sensei) in 2006.

EmptyHands Karate School fosters an inclusive and welcoming environment for everyone. Our mission is to provide a safe space where people can learn and grow through Martial Arts, Healing and Bodywork.

Since our establishment we have proudly taught hundreds of students, helping them achieve their personal and martial arts goals. Our commitment to excellence is evident in the black belts we have awarded over the years.

We understand that martial arts involves more than just physical skills — it’s also about building confidence, discipline and a sense of community. Our students often describe their experience as life-changing, highlighting the self-esteem and resilience they have gained through their training.

  1. 1994Azeem begins formal Karate training as a founding member of an East London dojo — Poon Dojo, Stratford.
  2. 1990sLaunches ZenKarate, one of the first online Karate magazines.
  3. 1999Begins studying Tai Chi Chuan with Master John Ding.
  4. 2003Graduates from the British School of Shiatsu-Dô and founds the EmptyHands clinic.
  5. 2006EmptyHands Dojo opens its doors in Ilford.
  6. 2011First Yudansha (black belt) awarded.
  7. 2013MA in Coaching (Oxford Brookes) — the first Karate Coaching Model.
  8. 2020The Virtual Dojo keeps students training together through lockdown.
  9. TodayKarate, Tai Chi, Yoga, Shiatsu & Coaching across Ilford and Epping.
What we offer

More than self-defence.

Tailored coaching sessions

Workshops and seminars designed around our students’ needs — Karate for self-defence, discipline and personal growth; popular Self-Defence Scenarios workshops; and Coaching for Mental Health sessions that address the psychological side of martial arts.

Holistic wellbeing

Shiatsu (acupressure) self-care sessions promote relaxation and stress relief through ancient healing techniques, while Tai Chi Chuan enhances flexibility, balance and mindfulness for all ages and fitness levels.

Rehabilitation & self-development

Whether you are recovering from an injury or seeking personal growth, we tailor our coaching to each individual. Reach out to us in confidence — we’re here to listen and to guide.

Community

The friendships matter as much as the techniques.

At EmptyHands we pride ourselves on fostering a strong sense of community. Our students frequently take part in local events, demonstrations and charity initiatives — including our annual Charity Cup — connecting with others and giving back.

Research indicates that martial arts training can enhance physical and mental health — see this study on martial arts and mental well-being.

Whether you are a beginner or an experienced practitioner, you will find a welcoming space to grow and learn. Our community is diverse, and we celebrate each individual’s journey.
