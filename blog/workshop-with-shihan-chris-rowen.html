Workshop with Shihan Chris Rowen

By Azeem Mushtaq Sensei & the EmptyHands team

Join us for an exclusive karate workshop with the renowned Shihan Chris Rowen, a 7th Dan black belt. With over 30 years of experience in martial arts, Shihan Rowen brings a wealth of knowledge. This workshop offers karateka of all levels the chance to deepen their understanding of traditional Shotokan Karate. You’ll explore techniques, philosophy, and discipline under the guidance of one of the world’s most respected instructors.

What to Expect:

  • Advanced Techniques: Learn refined kata applications (bunkai) and explore the technical aspects of striking, blocking, and stance work. Shihan Chris Rowen will guide you through practical drills that enhance precision, power, and speed.
  • Karate Philosophy: Shihan Chris Rowen will share insights into the deeper philosophy of karate. He will focus on applying the principles of perseverance, respect, and mindfulness.These values are essential both on and off the dojo floor. Consequently, they help cultivate a respectful environment in training and everyday life. Furthermore, practicing these principles not only fosters stronger relationships among students and instructors but also enhances personal growth. Moreover, when students embrace these values, they create a supportive community that benefits everyone involved. In addition, this sense of community promotes collaboration and mutual respect, making the dojo a welcoming space for all. Ultimately, these principles contribute to a more enriching martial arts experience.Ultimately, this environment encourages continuous improvement and lifelong learning.
  • Personalized Instruction: Whether you’re a beginner or advanced, Shihan Rowen’s keen eye for detail will help you. As a result, his guidance will improve your form and execution. Additionally, he offers personalized feedback to push your abilities even further. Therefore, you can expect significant growth in your skills during the workshop.
  • Sparring & Kumite Strategies: Discover new sparring tactics and methods. Improve your reaction time, distance control, and mental focus during kumite sessions.

Why Attend:

This workshop offers a unique opportunity to train with one of the most experienced instructors in Shotokan karate. Shihan Chris Rowen blends traditional karate values with modern applications. His teaching ensures that each participant improves their techniques and gains a better understanding of kata. They will also develop the mental fortitude to advance their karate journey.

About Shihan Chris Rowen:

Shihan Chris Rowen has been practicing Shotokan karate for over three decades. He is well-known for his dedication to preserving the art’s authenticity while pushing the boundaries of modern training techniques. Shihan Rowen has taught students worldwide. His workshops are praised for clear instruction, inspirational leadership, and a strong commitment to the spirit of karate-do.
