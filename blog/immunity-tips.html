Immunity Boost: 7 Powerful Tips for a Healthier You

By Azeem Mushtaq Sensei & the EmptyHands team

Strengthening Immunity with Traditional Asian Medicine

In today’s fast-paced world, accessing local healthcare providers can feel daunting. Fortunately, we can support our immune system through Traditional Asian Medicine (TAM), a holistic health approach practiced for over 5,000 years. This ancient system uses various tools, such as acupuncture, acupressure, and dietary adjustments. Together, these methods restore balance and promote healing in both the mind and body.

TAM emphasizes the interconnectedness of bodily systems and suggests that physical health deeply intertwines with mental well-being. TAM focuses on the body’s energy systems and immunity to enhance overall vitality and immune function.

Kidney 27: A Gateway to Immune Health

Location:
Kidney 27 is an essential acupressure point located about one inch from the midline on the lower border of the collarbone. As a result, it is easily accessible. In Traditional Asian Medicine, this point is significant for strengthening the body’s defenses and immunity.

Benefits:
This immune-boosting point offers various health benefits, particularly in alleviating upper respiratory flu symptoms, shortness of breath, and fatigue.

The Kidney system actively grasps Lung Qi, distributing the air that enters the lungs throughout the body. Stimulating Kidney 27 enhances respiratory function and boosts energy levels, making it invaluable for those feeling run down or unwell.

Lung 7: A Vital Point for Respiratory Relief

Location:
Lung 7 serves multiple functions in health and wellness. People widely recognize this point for its immunity ability to provide systematic relief from coughs and sore throats. As a result, it is particularly beneficial during cold and flu season. To locate this point easily, make a thumbs-up sign. Then, find the spot just above where your two fingers touch the inside of your wrist. This point is located on the radial margin of the forearm, above the styloid process of the radius.

Benefits:
Lung 7 serves multiple functions in health and wellness. People widely recognize this point for its immunity ability to provide systematic relief from coughs and sore throats. Therefore, it is particularly beneficial during cold and flu season. Additionally, you can use this point to boost defensive Qi and enhance the immune system’s functionality.

By stimulating Lung 7, individuals may find relief from various issues. These include headaches caused by external pathogens, neck rigidity, facial paralysis, toothaches, asthma, and nasal problems.

Large Intestine 11: The Fever Reducer

Location:
Located at the elbow, Large Intestine 11 is easily identifiable. To locate it simply, bend your arm and look for the end of the crease on the outer side of your bent elbow. Additionally, you can feel for the point by running your fingers along the crease to find the exact spot.

Ultimately, this method makes it easy to identify Lung 7 accurately. Additionally, this point is significant in Traditional Asian Medicine and plays a crucial role in enhancing immune response.

Benefits:
Known as a fever-reducing point, Large Intestine 11 serves as a key treatment for colds and influenza. It also acts as a preventative measure against immune-compromising conditions. By applying pressure to this point, one can help regulate body temperature and promote overall wellness. Practitioners often use this point in conjunction with other acupressure techniques to create a comprehensive approach to combating illnesses.

Integrating Traditional Asian Medicine into Daily Life

Incorporating Traditional Asian Medicine into your daily routine can be a transformative experience. By understanding the locations and benefits of key acupressure points such as Kidney 27, Lung 7, and Large Intestine 11, individuals can actively participate in managing their health and immunity. Furthermore, this knowledge empowers them to use these techniques effectively in their daily wellness routines. Simple practices such as gentle pressure or massage on these points can enhance energy flow and bolster the immune system.

Additionally, combining these techniques with a balanced diet rich in whole foods and adequate hydration can further enhance your body’s natural defenses. In addition, incorporating regular exercise and stress management practices can significantly boost overall wellness. Consider integrating herbal remedies known in Traditional Asian Medicine to promote healing and vitality.

Conclusion

In summary, Traditional Asian Medicine offers invaluable tools for maintaining and enhancing immune health. By focusing on critical points such as Kidney 27, Lung 7, and Large Intestine 11, individuals can take proactive steps in their wellness journey. Whether through acupuncture, acupressure, or dietary choices, embracing the principles of Traditional Asian Medicine (TAM) can lead to a more balanced and healthier life.
