The virtual dojo

By Azeem Mushtaq Sensei & the EmptyHands team

The EmptyHands dojo does not stop at the doors of the training hall. Alongside weekly classes, members can join the Virtual Dojo, an online space for live training, learning resources and conversation.

How it began

The Virtual Dojo runs on Microsoft Teams. It came into its own during the 2020 lockdown, when training halls closed and every Thursday class moved online. Students kept training together, supported one another and held on to routines that had become an important part of their lives. Read how the online dojo was built.

What members get

  • Free online training sessions.
  • The syllabus, training notes and videos in one place.
  • A place to ask questions and get feedback from instructors and fellow karateka.
  • Support between gradings, from belt to belt.

The Virtual Dojo is free for all EmptyHands members.

How to join

  1. Email info@emptyhands.co.uk with the email address you would like to use.
  2. You will receive an invitation to join the EmptyHands team.
  3. Follow Microsoft’s guide to joining a team as a guest.

Students can also use the EmptyHands Portal for the dojo calendar, notices from Sensei, homework and fees.
